About Us

Connecting passionate organisers with caring supporters across the Gold Coast and Brisbane.

Our Mission

Charity Events Gold Coast was founded in 2020 with a simple mission: to make it easy for people to discover and support local charity events. What started as a small Facebook group has grown into a platform connecting thousands of volunteers with over 50 charity organisations across South East Queensland.

We believe that every small act of kindness adds up. Whether you're joining a fun run, attending a gala dinner, or bidding in a silent auction — your participation makes a real difference to the causes you care about.

Our Values

🌱 Community First — We believe in the power of community to create change.

🤝 Transparency — 100% of ticket proceeds go directly to the charity.

💛 Compassion — We support causes big and small, from animal rescue to mental health.

✨ Inclusion — Events for everyone, every age, every background.
