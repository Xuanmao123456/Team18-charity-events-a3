About Our Platform

Charity Events Gold Coast connects passionate organisers with caring supporters. We partner with leading charities including Heart Foundation, Cancer Council QLD, RSPCA QLD, Starlight Children's Foundation and Beyond Blue.

Whether you're joining a fun run, attending a gala dinner, or bidding in a silent auction — your participation makes a real impact. Contact us at hello@charityevents.org.au or call (07) 5555 0000.
